Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Coursion Studio builds software that does its work on your device whenever it can. This page covers this website and the studio as a whole. Each app also has its own privacy policy with the specifics, linked from its website or App Store page.

This website

coursion.studio is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no advertising scripts.

We do count visits. A small script sends the address of the page you opened and the site that referred you to our own page-view counter. It stores nothing in your browser and does not try to identify you. GitHub, as the host, receives standard web request data such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Our apps

  • By default, our apps keep your files and data on your device.
  • When a feature needs the network, for example asking an AI provider you configured with your own key, the request goes straight from your device to that provider. We are not in the middle.
  • Some apps, such as Hutch, send anonymous crash reports and feature-usage counts so we can fix problems. You can switch this off in the app's settings.
