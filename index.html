How we work

The rules we come back to every time we start something new.

Your device first

If a job can run on your Mac or phone, that's where it runs. Your files and data don't take a detour through a server you never asked for.

Native, whatever the stack

SwiftUI on the Mac. React Native and Expo on mobile, with Swift and Kotlin wherever a real platform API is the honest answer. Whatever sits underneath, the app should feel quick, quiet and at home.

Pay once where we can

Our Mac apps are free or a one-time purchase. When an app has a running cost behind it, like AI features in our mobile apps, it may use a subscription, and the App Store page says so up front.

Range over a single bet

One studio, many apps: utilities, developer tools, health, productivity and lifestyle. Good ideas rarely stay in one category, and shipping across them keeps the work honest.

A question, a bug or an idea?

Email goes straight to the people who build the apps. Press and partnership requests are welcome too.
